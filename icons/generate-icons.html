PWA Icon Generator

Ready to generate icons from icon.svg
How to use:
  1. Click "Generate All PNG Icons" to create all required sizes
  2. Click "Download All as ZIP" to save them to your computer
  3. Extract the ZIP into the icons/ folder
  4. Deploy to Netlify

The SVG icon is already referenced in manifest.json and works in modern browsers. PNG icons are for maximum compatibility with older devices.
